Module Summary¶

Key Concepts¶

The big picture¶

Key insights to carry forward¶

  • Optimisation is how networks learn by updating parameters; it is more than applying gradients.
  • Mini-batch SGD is the default; batch size affects stability, speed and noise.
  • Momentum reduces zig-zag and speeds progress in consistent directions.
  • RMSProp and Adam adjust learning rates automatically and work very well in early and noisy training.
  • A fixed learning rate is limiting; schedules (step decay, cosine annealing, warm-up) match the step size to each phase of training.
  • Gradient clipping, awareness of pitfalls, and reading noisy vs smooth convergence correctly keep training reliable.
  • ★ Optimisation is about training dynamics: choose the optimiser, learning rate and schedule together. Stability tools prevent failure; they do not guarantee success.

What's next — regularisation and generalisation¶

  • Preventing overfitting and building models that perform well on unseen data.
  • Techniques: weight decay, dropout, early stopping, data augmentation.
