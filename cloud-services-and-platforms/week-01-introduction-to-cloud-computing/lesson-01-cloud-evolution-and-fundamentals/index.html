Cloud Evolution & Fundamentals¶

Key Concepts¶

1. Cloud Evolution¶

flowchart LR
    A["1960s<br/>Sharing Computers"] --> B["1990s<br/>Virtualisation<br/>(one physical server → multiple VMs)"]
    B --> C["2006<br/>AWS — renting servers,<br/>one-click provisioning, no upfront costs"]
    C --> D["2010<br/>Microsoft Azure,<br/>Google Cloud"]

2. Cloud & Key Traits¶

What is Cloud Computing¶

  • Delivery of cloud services over the internet
  • Pay for what you use — instead of physically maintaining servers, you rent them when you need them
  • Increasing capacity is easy

Key Traits¶

  • Scalability — expand as needed
  • Elasticity — scale up or down based on usage
  • On-demand service

3. Why Cloud Matters Today¶

flowchart TD
    W["Why Cloud Matters Today"]
    W --> C["Cost Savings:<br/>pay for what you use — no huge<br/>maintenance/upgrade costs"]
    W --> F["Fast Innovation:<br/>infra available instantly, one click away<br/>— faster speed to market"]
    W --> S["Scalability & Elasticity"]
    W --> G["Global Reach:<br/>e.g. expanding to Europe doesn't require<br/>setting up physical data centres"]
    W --> R["Reliability & Availability:<br/>if one region goes down, traffic routes<br/>to another — fewer outages"]
    W --> SC["Security & Compliance"]
